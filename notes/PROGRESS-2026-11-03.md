# BioTerm-Bench progress report, 2026-11-03

Written for picking the project back up after a ten-week gap. The most recent
work before this was 2026-08-31.

## What BioTerm-Bench is

A benchmark asking whether an AI agent can do routine bioinformatics work in a
terminal. Each task gives the agent a Linux container with standard tools
(samtools, bcftools, minimap2, R, Python), some input files (FASTQ, BAM, VCF,
GTF and so on) and a short instruction such as "count reads per gene" or "find
de novo variants in this trio". A deterministic grader checks the output file.
Tasks run on the Harbor harness with the Terminus-2 agent.

The motivation, from the ECCB abstract, is to find out whether an open model
running on a lab's own or federated infrastructure (ELIXIR / e-INFRA CZ) can
handle the simple jobs a lab would otherwise give a bioinformatician. This
matters most for labs whose data cannot leave the building.

## v1.0 results so far

1,910 of 2,500 trials are scored, and 34 of 50 deployments are complete.
The scores below are single-attempt mean reward (k=1):

| mean reward | deployments |
|---|---|
| **1.000** | DeepSeek V4 Flash Thinking, GLM 5.2, Kimi K3, Qwen3.8-27B, Qwen3.8 Flash Next (all e-INFRA) |
| 0.94–0.98 | 11, including GPT-5.6 Luna, Kimi K2.5, GLM 5, Mistral Medium 3.5 |
| 0.80–0.92 | 12, including Gemma 4, DeepSeek V3.2, Qwen3.5 122B |
| below 0.80 | Mistral Small 0.74, GPT-OSS-120B 0.54–0.60, Ministral 14B 0.20, GPT-OSS-20B 0.16 |

The benchmark separates weak, middle and strong models well. Its top is
saturated, though: five deployments score perfectly. `notes/v1.0-freeze.md`
records this as a known v1.0 limitation.

The same weights can score differently depending on the provider. Qwen3.8-27B
scores 1.000 on e-INFRA and 0.94 on OpenRouter. Report these as separate
deployments; never average them.

The 16 unfinished deployments are all small or older OpenRouter models
(Mistral Small/Nemo, Qwen3 8B–32B, Qwen 2.5 7B, Nemotron Nano). They stalled
because the provider no longer serves them or they failed the smoke test. The
budget was not the cause: OpenRouter spend was $23.81 of the $150 cap. GLM 5.1
(38/50) and Qwen3.5 9B (37/50) are nearly done.

## Two calls for the author

- **`gtf-reconstruct-translate` is not in v1.0.** In August it was the
  strongest item available: the small probe models scored 0/5 and Qwen3.8-27B
  scored 3/3. No note explains why it was left out. Including it now would
  require a new benchmark version.
- **Three v1.0 tasks use exact-match graders**: `vcf-to-tsv`,
  `seqkit-fasta-stats` and `vcf-multisample-qc`. In August they rejected
  numerically correct answers that were formatted differently. Later,
  `scripts/check_grader_tolerant.sh` was changed to exempt them with the reason
  "contract requires exact formatting". Before publishing, confirm their
  instructions really do specify that format. Two of them were the weakest
  items in the earlier IRT fit.
